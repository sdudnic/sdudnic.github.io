# About the author

Sergiu Dudnic is a civic activist and patriot. On dudnic.com, he publishes documentation, analysis and opinions about the Republic of Moldova.

In an ironic register, he sometimes describes himself as “an agent of the Kremlin, the White House, the Élysée and the Knights Templar” — a satirical formula about an independent point of view and a refusal of simplistic labels.

Its public work follows the rule of law, the Moldovan language and the identity of the Moldovan state, historical memory, and citizens' right to informed participation.

The focus remains on sources, arguments and public-interest topics.
