  <h1>Electoral abuse by PAS and “EU 2028”</h1>
  <p class="page-lead">A concise file on electoral promises, voter information and the difference between a political target and a guaranteed result.</p>
  <div class="dossier-prose">
    <p>The PAS electoral programme for the 2025 parliamentary election set the objective of signing an EU accession treaty by 2028. This initiative treats the phrase as a public commitment to verify, not as a guaranteed outcome.</p>
    <p>Public criticism does not oppose Moldova’s European direction. It asks for precise distinctions between preparation, negotiations, treaty signature and actual accession, as well as clear accountability when a political deadline is not met.</p>
    <p class="source-note">The primary documents and the detailed Moldovan-language file remain available for public verification.</p>
