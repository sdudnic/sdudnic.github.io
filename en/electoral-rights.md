  <h1>Electoral abuse by PAS and “EU 2028”</h1>
  <p class="page-lead">Voter information, accountability for public promises and the right to decide on the basis of verifiable facts.</p>
  <div class="dossier-prose">
    <p>Electoral rights do not end on election day. They include access to verifiable information, the ability to compare promises with results and the freedom to ask for explanations from those seeking a public mandate.</p>

    <h2>A reference point for verification: “EU 2028”</h2>
    <p>The PAS electoral programme for the 2025 parliamentary election set the objective of signing an EU accession treaty by 2028. The <a href="https://pas2025.md/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Programul-Electoral-PAS-UE-2028-redactat-2.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">PAS electoral programme</a> is the primary document to consult.</p>

    <p>Public criticism of this formula does not oppose Moldova’s European direction. The question is whether an electoral deadline should be presented as a certain result when the process depends on reforms, the closing of negotiations, and the agreement of EU institutions and member states. The <a href="https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/enlargement-policy/steps-towards-joining_en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">European Commission describes accession</a> as a process conditional on meeting the criteria, completing the chapters and securing the support of all EU governments.</p>

    <h2>Questions for any electoral promise</h2>
    <ul class="link-list">
      <li>What exactly is promised: preparation, signing the treaty or actual accession?</li>
      <li>Which conditions depend on the institutions of the Republic of Moldova, and which depend on the European Union?</li>
      <li>Which indicators and reports allow voters to verify progress?</li>
      <li>What happens if the deadline is not met, and who gives a public account?</li>
    </ul>
