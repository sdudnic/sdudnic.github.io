  <p class="eyebrow">Civic initiative</p>
  <h1>Facilitating Moldova’s approach to European standards</h1>
  <p class="page-lead">A direction for functioning institutions, protected rights and responsible public administration.</p>
  <div class="dossier-prose">
    <p>The initiative focuses on making Moldova’s approach to European standards more concrete: rule of law, accountable administration, rights protection and inclusive education.</p>
    <ul class="link-list"><li>institutions that explain and defend their decisions publicly;</li><li>rules and procedures accessible to citizens;</li><li>respect for historical, cultural and linguistic diversity;</li><li>public promises assessed through verifiable results.</li></ul>
    <p class="source-note">The MOLDOVA platform supports this direction through documentation, civic dialogue and public accountability.</p>
  </div>
