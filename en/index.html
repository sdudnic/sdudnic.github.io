A message for Moldova

A country with roots and a future

Moldova is not a temporary project. It is not a Russian colony, and it is not a Wallachian one. Moldova is and remains the core of an old, medieval, stable and steadfast state — a modern republic with dignified people who defend their independence, sovereignty and right to decide their own future.

We believe in a free, peaceful and strong Moldova, built by its citizens. We believe in the future of our country and in the power of its people to raise it through work, courage and solidarity. Moldova deserves hope, respect and pride.

About the platform

A space for documentation and public debate

This site brings together texts, sources and observations about Moldova, its past and its institutions. The platform is presented through its public themes and contributions rather than a detailed personal biography.

Materials combine documentation with editorial analysis. Sources, quotations and verifiable claims should be read in context, while opinions are presented as viewpoints for public debate.

About the author: Sergiu Dudnic is a civic activist and patriot. In an ironic register, he sometimes describes himself as “an agent of the Kremlin, the White House, the Élysée and the Knights Templar” — a satirical formula about an independent point of view and a refusal of simplistic labels.
