## Petition on the History of "Romanians" in Moldova

### Chronological Description of Events for Administrative Action

1. **09.10.2019** - The first email was sent to the Council for the Prevention and Elimination of Discrimination and Ensuring Equality of the Republic of Moldova. The petition was registered under number 1526, requesting an assessment of whether the name of the subject "History of Romanians" constitutes ethnic discrimination.
   
2. **15.10.2019** - The Council confirms the registration of the petition under number 1526 and informs that the review will be communicated additionally, asking for receipt confirmation.

3. **28.10.2019** - The Council suspends the review of the petition registered on 09.10.2019 under number 1526, to provide additional materials.

4. **12.07.2020** - The initiative group requests the continuation of the review of petition no. 1526.

5. **14.07.2020** - The Council responds, informing that the complaint has been entered into the registry of complaints and self-referrals, assigned case number 150/20.

6. **14.04.2024** - The initiative group sends a request to the Chairman of the Council for Equality, Ian Feldman, asking for an official response to petition no. 1526 / 150/20. It is stated that no response has been received for 4 years and 184 days, and an official decision is requested within 15 days.

7. **02.05.2024** - The Council for Equality responds, mentioning that a new composition of the Council was appointed on 28.03.2024, and they need time to study all the circumstances, after which a decision will be made and sent.

8. **10.05.2024** - The initiative group sends a new email to the Council for Equality and other relevant institutions, emphasizing the following points:
    - The petition was submitted almost five years ago, and the legal resolution period has been exceeded.
    - Chairman Ian Feldman is the principal rapporteur, and responsibility has not changed.
    - The reasons cited for the delay are irrelevant, emphasizing that the Council should be able to analyze the case without external experts.
    - The essence of the petition is clear: "Is the name of the mandatory school subject for all students of any ethnic origin in the Republic of Moldova 'History of Romanians' discriminatory?"
    - The necessary materials for analysis have already been provided by the initiators, and Parliament and other institutions requested answers without success.
    - The Council for Equality has not responded to multiple requests and questions.

### Conclusion
The Council for the Prevention and Elimination of Discrimination and Ensuring Equality has delayed the decision-making process on the petition regarding the name of the subject "History of Romanians" for almost five years. Numerous requests for answers were ignored, and the delay is considered deliberate and interested. This forms the basis for an administrative action against the Council for Equality for unjustified delay in the decision-making process.

### Note
At the time of filing the application, the law regarding the Council for Equality required a response within 10 working days, with a maximum of 45 days from the date of the request. In the current version of the law, the term has been extended to a maximum of 90 days. The laws in question are:
- [LAW No. 298 on the activity of the Council for the Prevention and Elimination of Discrimination and Ensuring Equality from 21.12.2012](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=110540&lang=ro)
- [LAW No. 121 on Ensuring Equality from 25.05.2012](https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=141518&lang=ro)
